# Mathematics for Computer Science

A structured, progressive roadmap for developing the mathematical foundations essential to Computer Science.

This repository covers mathematics from fundamental arithmetic and algebra to university-level topics, emphasizing logical reasoning, analytical thinking, and problem-solving.

**Learning Path:** Basic Mathematics → Advanced Mathematics → Discrete Mathematics → University-Level Mathematics

**Objectives**
- Build a strong foundation in mathematical concepts and techniques.
- Develop logical reasoning and systematic problem-solving skills.
- Understand the mathematical principles behind programming, data structures, and algorithms.
- Understand the mathematical principles underlying Computer Science, Data Science, and Artificial Intelligence.
