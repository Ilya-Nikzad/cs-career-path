# Numbers and Basic Arithmetic

## 1. Counting and Number Recognition

1-Counting Numbers: 

Counting is the process of saying numbers in a specific order to determine how many objects are in,
a group. The counting sequence begins with 1 and continues as 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, and so on.

2-Number Recognition:

Number recognition means identifying a number by its written symbol, spoken name, or quantity.

Numbers can be represented in different ways.
- Numeral: the written symbol, such as 7.
- Number name: the word, such as seven.
- Quantity: the amount represented, such as seven objects.

3-Counting Forward and Backward:

Counting forward helps you find the next number. Counting backward helps you find the previous number and prepares you for subtraction.
- The number after 12 is 13.
- The number before 12 is 11.

4 Comparing Numbers:

Comparing numbers means determining which number is greater, smaller, or equal.
