# `Comparison Operators`
---

Comparison operators compare values and produce a Boolean result: True and False

Operator Meaning:

The `==` equal to

The `!=` not equal to

The `>` greater than

The `<` less than

The `>=` greater than or equal to

The `<=` less than or equal to

---

# Important Rule — Comparison Operators


- **Return:** `True` or `False` 


- `==` → equal 


- `!=` → not equal 


- `>` → greater than 


- `<` → less than 


- `>=` → greater than or equal 


- `<=` → less than or equal 


- `=` is **assignment**, not comparison 


- `==` is commonly used inside `if` conditions 


- Comparisons can be used with numbers, strings, and other comparable values.
